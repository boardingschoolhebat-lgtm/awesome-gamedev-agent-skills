## Cut ladder

1. Optional modes and cosmetics.
2. Content volume (levels, items, enemies).
3. Platforms and languages.
4. Secondary polish (VFX, voice, cinematics).
5. Ending scope (last resort).

- Protect: core loop, save reliability, accessibility basics, cert blockers.

## Cadence

- Weekly: playable build, playtest notes, bug triage.
- Biweekly: demo, scope vs plan, risk review.
- Milestone: go/no-go, retrospective, re-plan.

## Production checklist

1. Three pillars written and used in decisions.
2. Minimum shippable game defined.
3. Vertical slice is final-quality and fun.
4. Milestones have dates and exit criteria.
5. Buffer 15–25 % exists.
6. Tasks are ≤3 days and estimated in ranges.
7. Top risks have mitigations and owners.
8. Bug triage has severity, owner and repro.
9. Cut ladder agreed before the crisis.
10. Content/localization freeze and launch plan exist.
